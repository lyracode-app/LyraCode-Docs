# Archive and restore

Archiving removes conversations or projects from everyday lists while retaining their history locally. See [backup and migration](/en/manual/data) to preserve data outside the device.

## Archive a conversation or project

1. Open the item's menu in the sidebar.
2. Archive it and confirm it leaves the regular list.
3. Open Archive in the sidebar and locate it there.

Project archiving affects the visibility of its conversations. Projects and conversations store separate archive flags; separately archived conversations still require restoration after their project is restored. Archiving does not copy, move or roll back project files, or stop background processes.

## Find and restore history

### Search

The archive lists projects and conversations. Search filters conversation titles, models or project names. Clear the query and check the regular list if an item is missing.

### Restore

Use the item's restore action and verify that it returns to the sidebar. Open it to check history, model and workspace. Directory grants or remote credentials may need reconfiguration after migration.

## Permanently delete

Deletion requires confirmation. Deleting an archived conversation removes its history; deleting a project affects its conversations. Back up anything needed first. Removing conversation records does not remove workspace files; see [file management](/en/manual/files).

## Verification example

Create a conversation called “Archive test”, archive it, search for it and restore it. Confirm its messages and workspace before using the workflow on real tasks.
