# Chapter 5: Managing Conversations — history, archive and projects

> This chapter guides you through managing your conversation records in Lyra Code.
>
> Reading time: about 5 minutes.

---

## 5.1 Conversation history

Open the sidebar and you'll see the **conversation history** here.

- **Tap a conversation**: enter its chat page and continue the conversation.
- **Long-press a conversation record**: opens the conversation menu, where you can:
  - Rename the conversation
  - Pin it
  - Archive it
  - Delete it

## 5.2 Archiving and deleting conversations

- **Archiving** does not delete your conversations — they're collected in the **archive page**.
- You can **restore** or **permanently delete** conversations anytime on the archive page.

::: tip Suggestion
Use archiving to keep your history list tidy.
:::

## 5.3 Projects

**Projects** exist to maintain the same working directory across multiple conversations.

The overall usage logic is the same as conversations, but with an extra **directory level**.
